\subsection{Evaporation directives\label{subsec:Evaporation-directives}}

The evaporation directives listed in Table~\ref{tab:inputlist} activate
and parameterize the model described in Section~\ref{sec:Evaporation}:
\begin{description}
 \item[\texttt{evaporation yes}] activate solvent evaporation and the
 concentration-dependent rheology (default: no; \texttt{evaporation no}
 is also accepted). With \texttt{evaporation yes} the directives
 \texttt{polymer frac}, \texttt{airviscosity}, \texttt{temperature},
 \texttt{umidity}, \texttt{bconstant} and \texttt{mconstant} are
 mandatory: a missing one gives the warning listed below and the run
 stops.
 \item[\texttt{evaporation polymer frac} $f$] initial polymer mass
 fraction $c_{p,0}$ (mandatory, warning 97). It must satisfy
 $0<c_{p,0}<1$, which is not checked; for $c_{p,0}\geq0.9$ no solvent
 evaporates (Eq.~\ref{eq:evap-evlim}).
 \item[\texttt{evaporation airviscosity} $f$] kinematic viscosity of
 air $\nu_a$ in $\mathrm{cm^2/s}$ (mandatory, warning 98). It is
 independent of \texttt{airdrag airviscosity}, which enters only the
 aerodynamic drag.
 \item[\texttt{evaporation temperature} $f$] temperature in kelvin
 (mandatory, warning 99, also when \texttt{relumidity} and
 \texttt{diffusivity} are both given).
 \item[\texttt{evaporation umidity} $f$] relative humidity $RH$ in the
 interval $[0,1]$ (mandatory, warning 100; a value outside the interval
 gives warning 106 and the run stops). The historical spelling
 \texttt{umidity} is retained for input-file compatibility.
 \item[\texttt{evaporation diffusivity} $f$] vapour diffusion coefficient
 $D_a$ in $\mathrm{cm^2/s}$. If omitted,
 Eq.~\ref{eq:evap-default-diffusivity} for water vapour is used at one
 atmosphere, and the value is printed as warning 105.
 \item[\texttt{evaporation relumidity} $f$] optional value of the
 saturation-vapour ratio $c_{s,\mathrm{eq}}(T)$. If omitted, it is
 evaluated for water from \texttt{evaporation temperature}
 (Eq.~\ref{eq:evap-psat}), and the value is printed as warning 102.
 The historical keyword is retained for input-file compatibility.
 \item[\texttt{evaporation bconstant} $f$] parameter $B$ in
 Eq.~\ref{eq:evap-viscosity} (mandatory, warning 103).
 \item[\texttt{evaporation mconstant} $f$] exponent $m$ in
 Eq.~\ref{eq:evap-viscosity} (mandatory, warning 104).
 \item[\texttt{evaporation tconstant} $f$] exponent
 $t_{\mathrm{ev}}$ in the generalized relaxation-time law; use $f=1$
 to reproduce Yarin et al. (2001) (default: 1).
\end{description}
