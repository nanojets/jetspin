\section{Test Case 13: dynamic-topology GPU baseline}

Test Case 13 initializes 1,024 beads uniformly between the nozzle and
collector and enables both \texttt{inserting yes} and \texttt{removing yes}.
It retains the material parameters and constant axial field of Test Case 9.
An intentionally artificial nozzle velocity of 2,000 cm/s forces multiple
topology events during a short 1,000-step run. Its input is stored in
\texttt{examples/input-13/input.dat}.

The reference event sequence contains 13 insertions and 13 removals. CPU and
OpenACC executions perform every event at the same timestep, finish with
1,024 active beads, and remain between 1,023 and 1,025 active beads. A
structured \texttt{Topology event:} line is printed whenever insertion or
removal occurs.

This is the baseline for future persistent dynamic-data development.
Insertion, removal, capacity growth, and compaction currently execute on the
host, while accelerator force evaluations use call-scoped mappings. An
initial paired NVFORTRAN 24.3 and NVIDIA A30 measurement required 40.659696 s
on CPU and 3.834931 s with OpenACC. The topology streams were identical. The
sampled physical observables passed with relative tolerance $3\,10^{-4}$ and
absolute tolerance $10^{-6}$; curvature after repeated topology changes is
more sensitive to floating-point ordering than the fixed benchmarks.
